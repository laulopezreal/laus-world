## Merck in a nutshell (important context)

**Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany** is one of the world’s oldest science and technology companies (founded **1668**). It operates globally but is **family-owned**, which strongly influences its culture and long-term thinking.

⚠️ Important distinction:

- **Merck KGaA (Germany)** ≠ **Merck & Co. / MSD (US)**
    
- Outside the US & Canada, they use the **Merck** name; in the US/Canada they operate as **EMD**.
