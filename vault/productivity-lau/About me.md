

Laura López Real is an Enterprise Artificial Intelligence Architect at AstraZeneca, where she leads the design of AI and data architectures that accelerate biomedical research and ensure safe, compliant innovation across R&D.

With a background in biotechnology and bioinformatics, Laura began her career bridging science and computation — developing data pipelines, knowledge graphs, and analytics systems to make biological insights discoverable and actionable. Her technical foundation evolved into a passion for designing **enterprise-scale AI ecosystems** that combine scientific rigor with modern engineering principles.

Today, her work focuses on **agentic AI**, **retrieval-augmented architectures**, and **knowledge-driven reasoning systems** — enabling AI to collaborate with scientists, not just automate tasks. She also contributes to shaping AstraZeneca’s AI governance and digital strategy, ensuring innovation aligns with ethical, transparent, and auditable principles.

Beyond her role, Laura is deeply engaged in mentoring and open innovation communities, where she advocates for human-centered AI design and the use of technology to advance discovery and improve patient outcomes.
